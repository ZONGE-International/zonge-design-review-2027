Geophysical services

The right method.
A clearer picture.

Zonge applies electrical, electromagnetic, magnetic, and gravity methods to image subsurface geology and support exploration decisions.

CSAMT ↗MT / AMT ↗IP ↗TEM ↗Gravity ↗Drone magnetics ↗

Controlled-Source AMT (CSAMT)

CSAMT uses a controlled electromagnetic signal to map subsurface resistivity, revealing geologic structures, alteration zones, and groundwater boundaries.

Explore method ↗

Gravity

Gravity surveys map subsurface density contrasts for structural geology, basin characterization, void detection, and exploration targets.

Explore method ↗

Induced Polarization (IP)

IP surveys map chargeability and resistivity to investigate mineralization, alteration, geologic structures, and groundwater targets.

Explore method ↗

Magnetotellurics (MT/AMT)

MT and AMT use natural electromagnetic fields to image subsurface resistivity for mineral, geothermal, groundwater, and regional geologic investigations.

Explore method ↗

Transient Electromagnetics (TEM)

TEM measures the decay of an induced electromagnetic response to investigate conductive mineral targets, groundwater, and subsurface resistivity.

Explore method ↗

UAV (Drone) Magnetic Surveys

UAV magnetic surveys provide efficient, high-resolution mapping of magnetic contrasts, structures, alteration, and buried infrastructure.
